Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information Nova Vision Digital ("we", "us") collects through novavisiondigital.online and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit the order form or email us, we receive the details you choose to share:

  • Your name and business email address
  • Your brand name and product link
  • The service you want to order and your project details
  • Any product photos or files you send us during a project

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and discuss your order
  • To produce, deliver and revise work you order
  • To handle invoicing and payment communication

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to run the business, such as our website host and form or email provider, and payment platforms (PayPal, Payoneer or Wise) when you pay. These providers process data under their own policies.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising trackers. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this page.

Retention

We keep project communication and files for as long as needed to deliver and support the work, then delete or archive them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing novavisiondigital21@gmail.com.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
